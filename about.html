About AEC

Quality-driven architecture and engineering consultancy committed to delivering innovative solutions that exceed client expectations

Engineering Excellence Since Foundation

AEC is a quality-driven architecture and engineering consultancy based in Muscat, offering a comprehensive range of design and project management services. As a registered Riyada Company, we are committed to delivering innovative solutions that exceed client expectations.

Our strength lies in the exceptional competence, quality, and dedication of our team. Our strong network of external associates provides immediate access to a vast array of resources and expertise, enabling us to deliver comprehensive and unique solutions to our clients.

Global Expertise, Local Excellence

With over 100 man-years of collective expertise, our multinational team of technical experts and associates brings extensive international experience from the Middle East, UK, Europe, and Southeast Asia.

We combine architectural and engineering principles to optimize design solutions and meet client requirements across industrial, commercial, and infrastructure sectors throughout Oman.
